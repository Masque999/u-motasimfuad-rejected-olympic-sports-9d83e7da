# Rejected Olympic Sports

One line, at most 200 characters, pitching a sport the Olympics rejected.

Entry: `Rejected: competitive apologising. Judges docked points for the heartfelt 'sorry' at the starting line, the tearful 'my bad' at the finish, and the podium speech that was just 'sorry' again.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
